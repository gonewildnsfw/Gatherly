Gatherly is under development

This is an early access implementation, with private media sales and manual creator payouts. A real payment and content-unlock test remains outstanding.

The operator must finalize the terms, content rules, eligibility, seller obligations, refund and dispute process, privacy information and support contact before public launch. Do not upload unlawful content or material you do not have permission to share.

Paid photo/video listings start as private drafts and need operator review before purchase. No earnings or revenue are promised.

Members must be 18 or older. Account and payout details must be accurate and belong to you. A date-of-birth declaration is not verified proof of age.

Creator payout policy

Creators may request manual payouts to their own South African ZAR bank account or compatible crypto wallet. Luno is optional. Other countries’ bank payouts are not yet supported. Transfers are manually reviewed; saving details or requesting a payout does not send funds. Existing requests retain their recorded destinations.

Our operator may earn a Luno referral reward from eligible new customers using code AQ7HMT. Referral use and crypto purchases are optional and are not conditions for withdrawing Gatherly earnings. Luno determines reward eligibility; no reward is guaranteed. Existing Luno customers can use their existing account.
