Privacy notice — early access

Gatherly is under development. Google sign-in is awaiting activation. Before public registration, the operator must provide their identity, contact details and a complete privacy policy.

Your private account details

We collect name, surname, date of birth, country and mobile number during account setup. Creators also provide their residential address and bank or crypto payout details. These are accessible to the account holder and authorized Gatherly administrators for account operations, support and manual payouts. Administrator views of private member records are logged. Details are self-declared and are not identity verification.

Your display name, bio and profile photo are public. You may choose to also show your name and surname; this is off by default and can be changed in Account. Your birth date, address, mobile number, payout details, purchase history and earnings are never published on your profile.

Data used by this implementation

Supabase stores authentication details, public display names and bios, text posts, follows, likes, comments, saved posts and reports. Display names, bios, published posts, follows, likes and comments are public. Saved posts, reports and draft listings are restricted by database access policies.

Authentication sessions are kept in your browser to maintain sign-in. Signing out clears this app’s local session. Content deletion and account-deletion handling need to be completed before a public launch.

Private photos and videos can be purchased through hosted crypto checkout. Payment records and access entitlements are stored privately. Do not submit sensitive information to public posts. This notice describes the current implementation and is not a finalized production privacy policy.
